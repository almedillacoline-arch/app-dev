# My Favorite Series: *FRIENDS*
 
**FRIENDS** is an American sitcom created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman. The show follows six friends living in New York City as they navigate life, love, and friendship.
 
## Summary of the story
- The series revolves around a close-knit group of friends — **Rachel**, **Ross**, **Monica**, **Chandler**, **Joey**, and **Phoebe** — who share countless hilarious and heartfelt moments.
- It captures their adventures from their 20s to 30s, covering everything from relationships and careers to the ups and downs of adulthood.
 
## Main Characters
1. **Rachel Green** – Fashion enthusiast who grows from a spoiled rich girl into a strong, independent woman.  
2. **Ross Geller** – A paleontologist with a complicated love life (and multiple divorces).  
3. **Monica Geller** – A chef known for her competitive nature and obsessive cleanliness.  
4. **Chandler Bing** – Sarcastic and witty, working in statistical analysis and data reconfiguration.  
5. **Joey Tribbiani** – An aspiring actor with a big heart (and a big appetite).  
6. **Phoebe Buffay** – Quirky, free-spirited musician with a mysterious past.  
 
## Reason why I love watching FRIENDS
*Friends* is timeless! It’s funny, heartwarming, and full of memorable moments.  
The chemistry between the cast and the show’s blend of humor and emotion make it perfect to rewatch again and again.
